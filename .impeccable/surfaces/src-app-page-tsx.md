# Home map and community

## Scope

Home route (`/`), web, Operate. Groundwork helps Delhi residents locate drainage reports and participate in their follow-up.

## Audience and task

Delhi residents need to see what is happening near them, understand a report's status, and join the community discussion. The map is the primary working view; a distinct feed is the secondary browsing view. Keep reporting, searching, area filters, sorting, report selection, voting, discussion, report-photo demo flow, and budget-tracker navigation available. Preserve clear sample/demo labels and do not imply reports are sent to authorities.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Groundwork makes a city issue legible first in place, then in community context. It refuses to bury the map beneath a feed: Map opens by default, Community is a full second view.

**OWN-WORLD:** Apple-like glass morphology: crisp frosted-white surfaces with real translucency and backdrop blur over a cool, misted city map; cool blue-gray page fields; deep pine for the Groundwork identity and primary actions; semantic rust, amber, and sage for report states. Compact system typography, generous controls, fine translucent borders, soft offset depth. Glass groups working controls and reports so each remains readable against moving geography.

**STORY:** Residents orient on the city map, scan meaningful status markers, select an issue, then switch to Community to sort and discuss reports in a familiar Reddit-like voting feed. Clear labels keep demo material distinct from verified civic information.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A full-bleed Delhi map owns the opening screen. A slim floating glass masthead carries the Groundwork mark, Map/Community switch, search/area filter, budget link, and report action. A restrained glass issue summary presents the selected or most active report without covering the map. The map/feed switch opens a dedicated feed view with familiar Hot/Top/New sorting and vote rails; mobile uses a bottom glass navigation and a legible feed sheet.

**FORM:** User-pinned replacement world and first-surface experience; no ranked candidate list. `seed: none (pinned)` — “apple-like glass morphic design with reddit like UX experience and a map first interface with second reddit like view”.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Open decisions

No new product claims or live data sources are authorized. Current reports and budget figures remain sample/demo content.
