# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Delhi residents who want to report and track drainage issues in their city.

## Product Purpose

Groundwork helps Delhi residents follow drainage issues and public drainage spending. Community issue reporting and drainage budget accountability are equally central to the product.

## Positioning

The product brings resident reports and drainage budget/RTI information together, connecting neighborhood-level issues with public works accountability.

## Operating Context

The current app presents a Delhi map and community report feed, with a separate budget tracker that can generate an RTI application template for a selected zone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The repository currently contains a demo interface with sample resident reports, sample agency updates, illustrative budget allocations and targets, demo votes/reputation, and sample discussion threads.
- The report photo flow runs demo checks; the interface says reports are not sent to an authority.
- The RTI template is a starting point and includes placeholders that a user must complete.
- Source links are needed before budget figures and works updates can be published as factual information.
- The app uses a web stack based on Next.js, React, Mapbox, and Supabase packages. The presence of a dependency does not establish that a live service is configured.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the current app implementation. Its report entries and budget figures are marked demo, sample, or illustrative; no verified source links or real-world evidence are included in the current interface.

## Product Principles

- Treat community reporting and budget accountability as equally important parts of the product.
- Help residents connect a local drainage issue with its reported status and relevant public works information.
- Make the evidence status of reports, updates, and figures clear; do not present sample data as verified fact.
- Keep reporting and RTI tools useful without implying that a report was delivered to an authority or that an RTI application was filed.
